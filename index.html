Pack Shelf
How Pulls Work
Every pack opens 5 cards, rolled from that pack's own set. The last card in every pack is guaranteed Handy rarity or better (higher with Guaranteed Slot upgrades), so packs never feel like a total whiff. Pulling a duplicate converts it into Scrap instead of a second copy filling your binder — spend that Scrap on the Upgrades tab.

Limited Edition and above are hold-one-at-a-time. While one of those cards is sitting in your collection it can never be pulled again, so every high-rarity hit is a card you don't already have — you'll never burn a Mythic roll on a duplicate. If you sell one, it goes back into the pool and can turn up again later.
