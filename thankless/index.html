How to play

Move with WASD / arrows, or drag anywhere on the left of the screen.
Mend casts itself on the most wounded ally in range — if you have the mana. Mana is scarce. Picking up the motes your party drops refills it, so you have to go where the fighting is.
Mend keeps them standing, but your spells decide whether they win. 1 Surge heals. 2 Bless doubles the busiest ally’s damage. 3 Haste speeds everyone up and keeps Brom awake. 4 Fortify shields the party and makes Brom the only thing enemies can see. 5 Rouse wakes, calms and shoves. R Revive when someone falls. Keep two buffs rolling and the party is dangerous; let them lapse and it is not.
Every minute or so something happens: a shrine to hold, a bounty, a pilgrim to escort, a vigil over Brom, an ambush, a rain of motes. See it through for a reward. The party levels up too, each in their own way, and picks their own skills: passives, and actives they cast on their own.
Ghouls burrow and come up under someone. Wisps ignore the party and drink your mana on touch; keep away or let Pip shoot them.
Chests fall at 3:00 and 7:00, the Demon Knight drops one, and any kill might. Walk over a chest to pick one of three relics for the run. Hold both halves of a pair and the next chest fuses them into something better.
From two minutes in, elites arrive with an aura and one trick each: swift, armored, healer-hunting, bursting into imps, or singing Brom to sleep. Hexers lob bolts at you from a distance; keep moving.
Enemies that get close to a healer forget everything else. Brom snores. Pip panics. Vex casts through you. Nobody says thank you.
