# Gravemark — Grok prompt: the five classes and every monster

Paste the STYLE block, then request the images one at a time (Grok holds style
best one image per message). Attach `art/actor/hero-idle.png` to every class
request and say "same world, same lighting, same footing as this". Save each
file under the name given. Any square size 1024 or larger is fine: I scale
them to the game's 256 on install. Zip the lot.

---

STYLE (paste above every request):

Painterly dark-fantasy game character art in the manner of Path of Idle:
hand-painted, visible brush texture, confident hard edges on the silhouette,
soft interior shading. Cold, damp, cut-stone world. NOT vector, NOT pixel art,
NOT 3D render, NOT anime, NOT flat cel shading. One low warm key light from
the lower left against a cold blue-grey ambient, with a bright rim light on
the edges so the figure reads against a dark background. Dark overall; bone
white #e6e0cf is the brightest neutral; exactly ONE saturated accent colour
per character, glowing, never two. Three-quarter view, full body, whole
figure inside the frame with air above the head and below the feet, feet
resting on an invisible ground line at about 90% of the height. Flat solid
magenta #FF00FF background edge to edge. No text, no watermark, no border,
no ground, no scenery, no shadow on the ground.
