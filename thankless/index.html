How to play

Move with WASD / arrows, or drag anywhere on the left of the screen.
Mend casts itself on the most wounded ally in range — if you have the mana. Mana is scarce. Picking up the motes your party drops refills it, so you have to go where the fighting is.
Spells: 1 Surge · 2 Ward · 3 Rouse · 4 Revive. Tap the buttons on a phone.
Chests fall at 3:00 and 7:00, and the Demon Knight drops one. Walk over a chest to pick one of three relics for the run.
From two minutes in, elites arrive with an aura and one trick each: swift, armored, healer-hunting, bursting into imps, or singing Brom to sleep. Hexers lob bolts at you from a distance; keep moving.
Enemies that get close to a healer forget everything else. Brom snores. Pip panics. Vex casts through you. Nobody says thank you.
