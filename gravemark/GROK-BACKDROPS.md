# Gravemark — Grok prompt: the eighteen backdrops (post-apocalypse cut)

Same world as `GROK-CHARACTERS.md`: a sun-bleached, colourful post-apocalypse.
Eighteen wide paintings: twelve realms the squads fight through, the camp
(the parish), three special modes, the graveyard, and the title. Save each
under the name given. **16:9 landscape**, 1920x1080 or larger; I scale them to
the game's 1280x720 on install. Zip the lot. No reference needed.

---

STYLE (paste above every request):

Stylised painterly environment art for a colourful post-apocalyptic idle RPG:
hand-painted, visible brush texture, simplified bold shapes, strong saturated
colour, bright daylight or golden hour. Palette: sun-bleached bone and sand,
rust orange, weathered teal, faded denim blue, safety yellow, one neon accent
per scene. The old world reclaimed: cracked highways, dead cars, overgrown
malls, water towers, radio masts, billboards, scrap walls, wildflowers in the
concrete. NOT grim, NOT gothic, NOT night, NOT horror, NOT photoreal, NOT 3D
render, NOT pixel art. Fun, inviting, a place you would want to explore.
Wide 16:9 landscape composition. IMPORTANT: this is a battle stage. The lower
third of the image is open, level ground, uncluttered, in a calm mid-tone so
characters standing on it read clearly; every landmark, structure and point of
interest sits in the upper two thirds, in the middle distance and far
distance. No characters, no creatures, no text, no signs with readable
words, no watermark, no border.

THE TWELVE REALMS — in order of depth, each ten depths long. Each scene is
one clear idea and one accent colour, and gets slightly stranger and more
dangerous than the last.

 1. realm-1.jpg   THE PAUPERS' FIELD. A vast trailer park and camp ground on
    a sunny plain, rows of leaning caravans, washing lines, wildflowers in the
    dust, distant water tower. Warm, welcoming, the starting place.
    Accent: safety yellow.
 2. realm-2.jpg   SEXTON'S ROW. A suburban street of identical bungalows,
    every lawn a grave with a hand-painted marker, mailboxes for headstones,
    sprinklers still running. Accent: teal.
 3. realm-3.jpg   THE OSSUARY. The inside of a huge warehouse store, endless
    shelving racks stacked with neatly sorted bones and skulls in labelled
    plastic crates, skylights pouring sun in. Accent: cold cyan.
 4. realm-4.jpg   BARROWMOOR. Rolling hills that are actually landfill mounds
    under a skin of grass and frost, old fridges and tyres poking out, a
    sagging fence line, cold bright sky. Accent: cold cyan.
 5. realm-5.jpg   THE DROWNED CLOISTER. A flooded shopping mall seen from the
    dry upper walkway, escalators going down into clear teal water, a giant
    bell hanging over the atrium, fish among the mannequins. Accent: storm
    yellow.
 6. realm-6.jpg   ASHFALL NECROPOLIS. A burnt-out city block, blackened
    towers, ash falling like warm snow in orange light, embers still glowing
    in the rubble. Accent: neon orange.
 7. realm-7.jpg   THE TURNING STONES. A field of concrete highway pillars and
    standing road barriers arranged in a stone circle, all covered in bright
    graffiti, under a huge yellow sky. Accent: storm yellow.
 8. realm-8.jpg   HOLLOW RELIQUARY. A bank vault district, every vault door
    open and every vault empty, purple emergency lighting glowing from
    inside them, gold-leaf peeling. Accent: violet.
 9. realm-9.jpg   THE LONG WAKE. An endless funeral procession of abandoned
    cars along a highway that never ends, black tarps flapping, purple dusk
    light, the mourners long gone. Accent: violet.
10. realm-10.jpg  CAIRNSPIRE. A mountain of stacked scrap and cars piled into
    a spire that reaches the clouds, cranes frozen mid-lift, warm sunset
    behind it. Accent: neon orange.
11. realm-11.jpg  THE UNLIT VAULT. The inside of a vast bunker, a blast door
    the size of a house sealed from the inside, cold concrete lit only by
    violet strip lights and the glow of the door's wheel-lock. Accent: violet.
12. realm-12.jpg  THRESHOLD OF VIGILS. The end of the world: the highway ends
    at a cliff edge over a bright void, a gate of welded scrap stands across
    it, lightning in a purple sky beyond. Accent: violet, brightest of all.

THE CAMP AND THE SPECIAL PLACES

13. town.jpg      THE PARISH, the player's home camp. A scrap-walled
    settlement on a sunny hill: a repurposed church with a solar panel roof,
    a workshop, a lookout tower made from a crane, a water tower, a library in
    a bus, a memorial of stacked road signs, a vault dug into the hill, a
    gate. Roads and paths between them, bunting, campfire smoke. Cosy,
    lived-in, bright. This scene is NOT a battle stage: fill the whole frame
    with the settlement, spread the buildings across it with space between
    them, seen from slightly above. Accent: warm campfire orange.
14. tower.jpg     THE DIVINE TOWER. A colossal radio mast turned into a
    climbing tower, platforms and ladders spiralling up into a clear blue
    sky, clouds below the top. Battle stage on one platform. Accent: cold cyan.
15. dimension.jpg THE ALTERNATE DIMENSION. The trailer park of realm 1 but
    wrong: the sky is the wrong colour, the caravans float, the horizon
    bends, colours shifted to teal and magenta. Accent: magenta pink.
16. finality.jpg  FINALITY. Past the end of the world: fragments of highway
    floating in a bright white-gold void, the wreck of the sun on the horizon,
    calm and enormous. Accent: gold.
17. graveyard.jpg THE GRAVEYARD, where the fallen are remembered. A quiet,
    sunny junkyard with rows of car-bonnet headstones and windscreen-glass
    flowers, one tree, butterflies. Peaceful. Accent: teal.
18. title.jpg     THE TITLE SCREEN. The Reaver, Warden, Pyre, Stalker and
    Sexton seen from behind, small, standing on a highway looking out at the
    whole world above: the trailer park, the mall, the burnt city, the spire,
    the gate at the end, all in one sweeping view under a huge bright sky.
    This one scene MAY show the five characters, as silhouettes from behind.
    Leave the upper centre calm for the game's title text. Accent: gold.
