LEGEND FIGHT CLIPS — the true-form cinematic, one set per legend
================================================================

The fight plays in a portrait window (440x660 in the game, 2:3). Today each
legend has a 14-frame breach plus two stills. The new fight wants FIVE shots
per legend, four of them short video clips the game turns into frame
sequences, one a still. Generate them in Grok Imagine as VIDEO where it says
clip; deliver the mp4 (or a folder of frames, 24 per clip is plenty). Name
the files <legend>-<shot>.mp4, e.g. ironjaw-breach.mp4. I extract, crop,
convert to webp and wire them in.

GLOBAL STYLE (paste at the top of every prompt)
- "Portrait 2:3, 720x1080. Painted storybook style with soft edges, the same
  rendering as the water paintings in the reference. Camera low at the water,
  just above the surface, looking slightly up at the fish. The fish faces the
  camera. No people, no rod, no line, no text, no watermark. Water spray and
  droplets in every shot. Keep the SAME creature design in every shot of a
  legend: same colours, same scars, same proportions."
- Feed the matching water painting from docs/fishing-scene-reference.webp
  (dock) or your six scene paintings as the style reference for its legend.

THE FIVE SHOTS (same list for every legend; only the creature line changes)

  A. breach   (clip, 3s)  The legend erupts from calm water straight toward
                          the camera, rising to fill the frame, a wall of
                          spray behind it. Starts with only a wake, ends with
                          the fish at its highest, mouth open.
  B. fury     (clip, 3s, loopable)  The legend thrashing at the surface,
                          filling the middle of the frame, water churning,
                          head swinging left and right. Start and end on the
                          same pose so it loops.
  C. lunge    (clip, 1.5s)  The legend lunges AT the camera, jaws wide, and
                          the spray hits the lens. Fast.
  D. beaten   (still)     The legend exhausted at the surface, on its side,
                          gills working, calm water, first light. Dignified,
                          not gory.
  E. gone     (clip, 2s)  The legend turns and dives away, tail up, then only
                          rings on the water.

THE LEGENDS (the creature line; keep it word for word across all five shots)

1. Old Ironjaw — the dock
   "A colossal, ancient common carp, 60 pounds, bronze scales like armour
   plates, a torn tail fin and three old gaff scars on its jaw, lake water
   at midday with green hills behind."

2. The Marsh King — the reedy shallows
   "A monstrous alligator gar, 70 pounds, long armoured body, olive-green
   with black speckles, needle teeth, rising among lily pads and cattails in
   misty marsh water."

3. The Pale Hunter — the deep ledge
   "A great white shark, huge, two-toned: slate grey above, bone white
   below, black eye, rising from cold dark blue water under a grey sky."

4. The Rooster King — the coral reef
   "A giant crested reef fish, 100 pounds, a tall red-and-gold dorsal crest
   like a rooster's comb, iridescent scales, over turquoise water and living
   coral in warm sun."

5. The Black Phantom — the midnight water
   "A vast pitch-black catfish, 140 pounds, skin like wet obsidian that
   swallows the light, pale whiskers, silver eyes, at night on still black
   water under a sky of stars, the only light a low moon."

6. The Drowned King — the confluence
   "A colossal kraken-like octopus, the size of a rowing boat, drowned-green
   skin with barnacles and old rope caught on it, a rusted crown sunk into
   its head, rising where five rivers meet under a storm sky."

DELIVERY
- 5 files per legend, 30 total. mp4 is easiest; frames (jpg/png/webp) in a
  folder also fine.
- Don't worry about matching sizes exactly; I crop to 2:3 and scale.
- If a clip drifts (the creature changes mid-shot), regenerate that one shot
  rather than the set. Consistency across the five matters more than any one
  shot being perfect.
