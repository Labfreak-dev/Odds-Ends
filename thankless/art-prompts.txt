#   tile_   seamless ground, 256x256, edges blended, background kept

#   (else)  character/enemy sprite, keyed off magenta, trimmed, fitted to 128px

# Sprites are keyed on MAGENTA. Every sprite prompt must end with the magenta lock,
# and nothing in the design may be magenta or hot pink. Vex is lavender, not magenta.
