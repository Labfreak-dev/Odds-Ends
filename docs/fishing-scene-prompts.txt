FISHING SCENE PAINTINGS — one per water
=======================================

Reference: docs/fishing-scene-reference.webp (the current lake, 1200x1800).
Feed it to the generator as the layout reference for EVERY prompt below.

LAYOUT RULES (the game draws on top of the painting, so these must hold)
- Portrait 2:3. Deliver 1200x1800 or 1600x2400, jpg or png.
- The far shore's waterline sits at 45% from the top. Sky above it, water below.
- A wooden pier enters from the LEFT edge and ends about a third of the way
  across, its deck at 40-46% height. The angler is drawn by the game and sits
  on the END of that pier, so keep the deck flat and clear of clutter there.
- A rowing boat rests at the bottom-left, around 72-78% height.
- Keep the bottom 8% quiet: the cast button covers it.
- No people, no fish, no birds, no reeds: the game animates all of those.
- Painted, storybook style, soft edges, same rendering as the reference.
- Same camera, same horizon, same pier, same boat in every painting; only the
  place changes. Say "same composition as the reference image" in each prompt.

PROMPTS

1. The Old Dock (this is the reference; only regenerate it if you want a
   fresh take)
   "Same composition as the reference image: a calm lake at midday, wooden pier
   from the left, rowing boat bottom-left, green rolling hills on the far shore,
   fluffy clouds, warm sun, clear blue water. Storybook painting, soft edges."

2. Reedy Shallows
   "Same composition as the reference image, but a marsh: shallow olive-green
   water with lily pads and cattail beds along BOTH shores, a low morning mist
   lying on the water, willows and reeds on the far bank instead of hills, dragonfly
   light, muddy shallows near the boat. Wooden pier from the left, rowing boat
   bottom-left. Storybook painting, soft edges."

3. Deepwater Ledge
   "Same composition as the reference image, but a cold deep cove: a sheer dark
   slate cliff rises along the RIGHT side from the far shore down into the
   water, grey-blue deep water with long slow swells, a few gulls' worth of sky
   left empty, overcast pale light, foam at the cliff's foot. Wooden pier from
   the left, rowing boat bottom-left. Storybook painting, soft edges."

4. The Midnight Mark
   "Same composition as the reference image, but at night: a deep purple-black
   sky full of stars and a low moon, the water a dark violet with a faint
   glowing purple patch on the surface right of center, thin wisps of mist,
   silhouetted hills on the far shore, the pier lit by two small warm
   lanterns. Wooden pier from the left, rowing boat bottom-left. Storybook
   painting, soft edges."

5. The Coral Shelf
   "Same composition as the reference image, but a tropical reef: crystal
   turquoise water so clear the coral shows beneath it, pink, purple and orange
   coral heads along the bottom third, white sand bars, bright hard sunlight
   and light caustics on the water, a palm-fringed far shore. Wooden pier from
   the left, rowing boat bottom-left. Storybook painting, soft edges."

6. The Confluence
   "Same composition as the reference image, but where five waters meet: a
   great slow whirlpool turning in the middle distance right of center, the
   water streaked in five colours (amber, green, slate blue, violet, teal)
   spiralling into a dark eye, a stormy bruised sky with a break of light, the
   far shore half marsh and half cliff. Wooden pier from the left, rowing boat
   bottom-left. Storybook painting, soft edges."

DELIVERY
Name the files by spot id: dock, shallows, ledge, midnight, reef, confluence.
The build embeds the painting and samples the far-shore ridge line from it
(FE_RIDGE) so stars stay in the sky, so each new painting gets its own ridge
sample at build time. That wiring is on this side; send the six files.
