# Gravemark — Grok prompt: the five classes and every monster (post-apocalypse cut)

Direction: **less dark, more fun, better colour.** A sun-bleached post-apocalypse,
not a graveyard at night. The dead still rise, but they rise into daylight, rust
and neon. Think chunky, colourful, characterful figures with big readable
silhouettes: a game you smile at.

Paste the STYLE block, then request the images one at a time (Grok holds style
best one image per message). **Make the Reaver first.** Then attach the Reaver
to every other class request and say "same world, same rendering, same footing
as this". Monsters and bosses need no reference. Save each file under the name
given. Any square size 1024 or larger is fine: I scale them to the game's 256 on
install. Zip the lot.

---

STYLE (paste above every request):

Stylised painterly game character art for a colourful post-apocalyptic idle
RPG, in the spirit of Path of Idle's chunky, readable heroes. Hand-painted with
visible brush texture and clean hard edges on the silhouette, exaggerated
proportions: big hands, big boots, big weapons, heads slightly large, bodies
built from simple bold shapes. Bright daylight lighting: a warm sun from the
upper left, a cool sky-blue bounce from the right, strong saturated colour
everywhere. Palette: sun-bleached bone and sand, rust orange, weathered teal,
faded denim blue, safety yellow, hazard stripes, one neon accent per character.
Materials of the wasteland: scrap metal, duct tape, car parts, road signs,
salvaged sports gear, patched leather, rope, plastic. NOT grim, NOT gothic,
NOT horror, NOT pixel art, NOT 3D render, NOT anime, NOT photoreal. Fun and
confident, a little cheeky. Three-quarter view, full body, whole figure inside
the frame with air above the head and below the feet, feet resting on an
invisible ground line at about 90% of the height. Flat solid magenta #FF00FF
background edge to edge. No text, no watermark, no border, no ground, no
scenery, no shadow on the ground.
