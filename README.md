# Odds & Ends

Browser games served by GitHub Pages.

- **Odds & Ends** — the card/idle game: https://labfreak-dev.github.io/Odds-Ends/
- **Ironhold Idle** — a standalone idle RPG: https://labfreak-dev.github.io/Odds-Ends/ironhold/
- **Thankless** — Vampire Survivors with the worst party; you are only the healer: https://labfreak-dev.github.io/Odds-Ends/thankless/
- **Dead Man's Pull** — Loot Dungeon x Slots & Daggers: a side-scrolling dungeon crawl where the slot machine decides every step and stab: https://labfreak-dev.github.io/Odds-Ends/lootdaggers/
- **Glimmerdeep** — a creature auto-chess roguelite: buy, place, merge and evolve creatures through 24 rounds and four biomes: https://labfreak-dev.github.io/Odds-Ends/glimmerdeep/
- **Iron League** — a mercenary club and a real-time pit: compose a roster and play a five-match season: https://labfreak-dev.github.io/Odds-Ends/iron-league/
- **Labfreak Legends** — a Hearthstone-style card battler with 2,318 cards from every Labfreak game, a pack shop and a deck builder: https://labfreak-dev.github.io/Odds-Ends/legends/
- **Gearbound** — a backpack auto-battler: buy shaped gear, pack it into a shaped backpack, merge twins and line up neighbour bonuses, then watch every item fight on its own timer. Twelve rounds a chapter, three lives a run: https://labfreak-dev.github.io/Odds-Ends/gearbound/
- **Hollowmere** — a Unity mobile dungeon crawler (Fate-style town, endless floors, fishing and a companion; Diablo III-style loot, runes and Torment). Not on Pages — open [`hollowmere/`](hollowmere/README.md) in Unity 6.
- **Shared art packs** — Heroes99, Elements Core sheets, and BitFX premade FX: [`shared-assets/README.md`](shared-assets/README.md)
