Hollowmere title art. Original drawings for this project, released under CC0.

Drop replacements in this folder (same names) and rebuild. The player loads them
through Assets/Resources/UiDropIn.asset, which references these files.

  keyart_bg_0_sky.png
  keyart_bg_1_ridge.png
  keyart_bg_2_lake.png
  keyart_bg_3_fore.png
  keyart_bg_fog.png
  logo.png
  button_normal.png
  button_pressed.png
  button_primary.png

Button images are 9-sliced (24 px border on a 96 px plate).
If a file is missing, the title paints a procedural stand-in.
