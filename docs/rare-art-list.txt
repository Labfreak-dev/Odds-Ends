BACKGROUND (same for every card — mid-tier, simpler than the foil paintings):

A soft painterly studio backdrop: muted slate-blue to warm grey vertical
gradient, gentle vignette at the corners, faint cool light from the upper
left. No scenery, no props, no text — the background stays plain and
out-of-focus so the subject reads instantly at card size. Subject centered,
filling about 70% of a square frame, semi-realistic painted style with
visible brushwork, less detail and less drama than a hero portrait.
