Codex

Combat runs on its own. You coach: stance, gear, eat threshold, area, and when to pull the boss. Damage you deal trains your skills. Monsters drop gold, food, gear and chests. Weapons need Attack levels, armour needs Defence. Kill enough in an area to unlock its boss; beat the boss to open the next area. Upgrade favourite pieces at the Forge with scrap from salvaging.

Keep: raise the five buildings with gold and scrap for permanent perks. The Barracks hires a companion (hound, cleric or squire) who fights beside you.
Runes drop from monsters, bosses and the tower; three runes make a Runeword you inscribe onto a piece of gear for a permanent bonus.
Tower of Trials: endless floors from the World tab. Records every five floors pay a chest. Dying just sends you home.
Bounties: three posted daily on the World tab; claim rewards there, reroll ones you dislike.
Prayer: light prayers for accuracy, damage, defence, regen or protection. They drain points; bones from kills restore them (auto-bury by default).
Party: the Barracks holds up to three companions; slots 2 and 3 open at Barracks level 2 and 4.
Uniques & sets: 30 named weapons with their own bonuses and 10 four-piece armour sets with 2- and 4-piece bonuses. Gold-bordered in the vault, never auto-salvaged.
Ascension: at the Altar, once the Elder Wyrm is slain or you reach combat 70. Resets your champion for permanent shards and a harder, richer realm.
